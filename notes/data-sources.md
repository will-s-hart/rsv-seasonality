# Data sources and feasibility

Assessed 18 November 2026. This is a source audit, not a downloaded analysis dataset. Small public CSV/API responses were inspected to verify fields and coverage; climate retrieval and full ingestion remain to implement. See [literature](literature-review.md), [methodology](modelling-methodology.md), and [analysis plan](analysis-plan.md).

## Recommended starting dataset

Start with **Japanese prefecture-week RSV counts, 2013–2019**, paired with population-weighted ERA5 weather; reserve 2020–2023 for a separately modelled pandemic/recovery analysis. Reproduce the focal paper's island aggregates first, then exploit prefectural climate contrasts. Use **England from July 2017** as the planned transfer test, with a separate observation model. Obtain historical English test counts/denominators before treating its positivity as an incidence observation. The public sources support exploratory comparison now; they do not yet establish that the climate–transmission surface is identifiable.

The inspected upstream `main` CSV ends in 2023. A plot axis extending to 2024 does not establish 2024 observations: reconcile the manuscript's exact analysis window against its archived code/data version before claiming full replication. The 2013–2019 window above is our selected pre-pandemic subset.

## Harmonisation and covariates

- Derive humidity **at each hourly grid cell before averaging**. With air temperature `T`, dewpoint `Td`, saturation vapour-pressure function `es`, and pressure `p`: `e = es(Td)`, `RH = 100e/es(T)`, absolute humidity `= e/(Rv*T_kelvin)` (consistent SI units), and specific humidity `q = 0.622e/(p − 0.378e)`. State water/ice convention and output units. Temperature plus one humidity measure is the primary pair; RH, absolute humidity and specific humidity are not interchangeable independent predictors. [ECMWF RH example](https://era-explorer.climate.copernicus.eu/notebooks/content/humidity/humidity_monthly_climatology.html).
- Aggregate climate using population weights within matching prefectures/UK areas; compare area weighting. Preserve hourly data long enough to examine variability and nonlinear averaging. Match local calendar weeks and report-date definitions; explicitly handle week 53 and Japan/UK time zones.
- Obtain age-specific population/births and boundaries from [Japan Statistics Bureau](https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/jinsui/2.html) and [ONS population estimates](https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/populationandmigration/populationestimates/datasets/populationestimatesforukenglandandwalesscotlandandnorthernireland). The replication repository provides initial childcare/population inputs, but national childcare trends cannot identify prefectural mixing effects. Add school/childcare calendars, testing policy, COVID measures, and immunisation coverage as separate documented covariates.

## Breaks and first feasibility gate

1. **Japan:** antigen-test reimbursement expanded in October 2011; reported case eligibility is clinically suspected **and test-diagnosed**, through paediatric sentinels. Sentinel selection changed at **2025 week 15**, about 3,000 to 2,000 sites. The 2025 ARI virology system is a different observation stream. [Official epidemiology and methods](https://id-info.jihs.go.jp/surveillance/idwr/article/rsv/010/index.html). Exclude 2025 onward initially; normalising by sentinel count alone may not remove selection changes.
2. **Pandemic and prevention:** treat 2020–2022 disruption/recovery explicitly. UK RSV programmes began August/September 2024, varying by nation; recent infant hospitalisation series therefore require prevention effects in the observation/severity model. [UK surveillance report](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/surveillance-of-respiratory-syncytial-virus-winter-2025-to-2026/surveillance-of-respiratory-syncytial-virus-winter-2025-to-2026). Do not interpret a summer publication pause or missing value as zero transmission.
3. **Gate before joint inference:** retrieve one season per source, validate units/weeks and denominators, inspect climate-support overlap, then lock a source manifest (URL, original licence, version/commit, retrieval date, checksum, revisions and transformations). If consistent England numerator/denominator data remain unavailable, use England for cautiously qualified seasonality validation and fit the mechanistic discovery model primarily to Japan; Scotland provides a practical UK sensitivity analysis.
