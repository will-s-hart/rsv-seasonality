# Weekly RSV coverage and reporting QA, 2013–2019

These are provisional QA recommendations for the pinned public aggregate weekly series. The coverage counts below come from the source audit and export verification. Cloud checks of the summary command used synthetic inputs. [Source audit](surveillance-audit-2013-2019.md), [weekly export](weekly-observations-2013-2019.md), [official input verification](analysis-plan.md#audited-analysis-input-manifest).

## Weekly observation flags

| Field or state | Meaning in the export | Provisional handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NESID `coverage=present` with a positive `paediatric_sentinel_sites` count | The final tables give weekly RSV case notifications and the size of the paediatric sentinel network. A case count of `0` means zero notifications in the final table. The tables do not measure weekly RSV testing, reporting participation or catchment. | Retain numeric reports, including zeros. Examine the site-count series within each prefecture. Investigate abrupt changes or unusual counts around holidays against dated source evidence before proposing exclusions or an adjustment. |
| NESID blank `source_outlier` and `positivity` | These fields are inapplicable. The published network size alone maps to `ReportingUnknown` in the Julia interface. `ReportingComplete` and `ReportingIncomplete` require independent evidence of participation. | Preserve unknown reporting. Only consider excluding weeks for independently documented incomplete reporting, and report those weeks separately. Any coverage-adjustment sensitivity needs dated evidence at the appropriate prefecture-week scale and an explicit rule. |
| NREVSS `coverage=absent` (`AbsentWeek`) | The exporter represents an antigen grid week with no row in the historical file. Detections, tests, flag and positivity are all blank. The reason for the absence is unknown. | Keep the week on the QA calendar and report the dates and lengths of gaps. Leave detections and positivity missing. |
| NREVSS `coverage=present`, zero tests (`ZeroTestWeek`) | The source supplied a row with numeric zero tests and zero detections. The CDC flag is retained and positivity is undefined. | Keep the source row and tally it separately from absent rows and tested weeks. Leave positivity missing. |
| NREVSS `coverage=present`, positive tests (`TestedWeek`) | Detections and tests are for the same method. Zero detections with positive tests gives observed zero positivity. | Retain tested weeks as the starting comparison set within each method. Inspect thin weekly denominators by method and region. Prespecify any volume screen and report the resulting coverage before comparing sensitivity results. |
| NREVSS `source_outlier=1` or `0` | CDC flags weekly tests or detections that exceed its three-standard-deviation criterion within method, HHS region and surveillance year. `1` means flagged and `0` means unflagged by that rule. The flag describes counts and does not identify an erroneous row or establish complete reporting. | Review flagged weeks against adjacent weeks and both native count fields. Compare retention with a flagged-tested-week exclusion as a separate sensitivity. Report overlap with any volume screen. A blank flag means the CDC field is unavailable or inapplicable for that row. |

The pinned NESID export has **17,155** present prefecture-weeks with positive site counts. NREVSS PCR has **3,650** tested region-weeks and **11** flags. NREVSS antigen has **3,569** present region-weeks, including **8** zero-test rows (four each in Regions 2 and 7), and **33** flags across present rows. Of **81** absent antigen region-weeks, Region 2 accounts for three (week endings 16–30 June 2018) and Region 8 for 78 (7 July 2018–28 December 2019). The prior totals do not tabulate the overlap of antigen flags with zero-test rows. [Pinned coverage and dates](surveillance-audit-2013-2019.md#us-historical-nrevss), [CDC field definitions](https://data.cdc.gov/api/views/52kb-ccu2), [Julia observation interface](modelling-methodology.md#aggregate-observation-interface).

## Reporting and source changes to annotate

- **Japan.** The 2011 expansion of reimbursement for antigen testing preceded the audit window. The number of paediatric sites reporting at least one RSV case during a year grew over part of the study period. This annual information cannot establish which sites reported in a given week. The final 2013 counts supersede provisional releases. From 2018 week 9 the IDWR graph displays cases per sentinel. The export uses final counts and site denominators across that boundary, so annotate the date without screening the week on the basis of the display change. The final rate sheets include RSV in 2018–2019. Earlier ratios can be computed from the two count fields. A documented change in testing or notification practices would warrant separate review of its timing and scope. [Japan source audit](surveillance-audit-2013-2019.md#japan-idwr-and-nesid), [JIHS graph notice](https://id-info.jihs.go.jp/surveillance/idwr/graph/weekly/respiratory-syncytial-virus-infection/index.html), [NIID IASR, December 2018](https://id-info.jihs.go.jp/idsc/niid/images/idsc/iasr/39/466e.pdf).
- **US PCR and antigen.** CDC documents changes in testing practice and participation in the voluntary laboratory network. The audited export shows rising PCR test volume and declining antigen volume between 2013 and 2019. Complete PCR rows establish aggregate presence only. The public file has no weekly lab roster, lab counts or catchment information. Report the PCR and antigen test volumes and flags separately. If antigen is used as a sensitivity stream, show its full coverage and use the intersection of tested region-weeks for a paired PCR comparison. Report the weeks removed by each proposed screen, with source keys, method, inclusive dates and reasons. Changes in method mix and the antigen gaps alone cannot identify a dated reporting break or its cause. [US source audit](surveillance-audit-2013-2019.md#us-historical-nrevss), [CDC 2014–2017 analysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/67/wr/mm6702a4.htm), [CDC source metadata](https://data.cdc.gov/api/views/52kb-ccu2).

## Reproduce the descriptive table

Generate the checksum-verified [weekly export](weekly-observations-2013-2019.md#generate-the-csv), then run from the repository root.

```sh
python -B scripts/summarize_weekly_coverage.py data/processed/weekly_observations.csv
```

The read-only command writes CSV to stdout, with one row per source, method, region and source week year. `input_rows` includes explicit absent antigen grid rows. `zero_reports` counts NESID zero notifications. For NREVSS, `zero_detection_rows` counts tested weeks with zero detections, and the two `flagged_*` columns cross the native flag with tested and zero-test coverage states. Within each stream, the positive-denominator minimum, median and maximum use paediatric sites for NESID and reported tests for NREVSS. Inapplicable fields and summaries with no positive denominator are blank. The yearly grouping uses source week labels, independent of the CDC flag's surveillance-year grouping.

The report summarizes supplied rows. Use the upstream checksum-verified exporter for the full grid and source provenance. For saved tables, record the input CSV digest, code commit and whether the input was official or synthetic. The [synthetic exporter fixtures](../scripts/test_export_weekly_observations.py) exercise the same summary command, including a flagged zero-test row and an all-absent group. Fixture counts describe the test data. [Result conventions](../results/README.md).
