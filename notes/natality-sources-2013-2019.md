The [source manifest](../data/natality-sources.toml) records the official releases, scope and national controls for calendar-month live births. Expected coverage is 3,948 Japan prefecture-months and 4,284 US state/DC-months. The catalogue entries and published controls were reviewed on 23 November 2026. The Cloud proxy returned 403 for the official files and the WONDER request site. Input bytes, digest pins and observed coverage remain pending.

## Japan

Use the seven individual final Natality Volume 2 Table 2 CSVs. The catalogue dates are update dates for the tables. Japan's main final tables cover Japanese vital events in Japan and publish events for foreign nationals in Japan, events for Japanese nationals abroad and prior-year events separately. The resident-population denominator from a separate source may cover additional nationalities. [MHLW survey scope](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/toukei/saikin/hw/jinkou/kakutei19/dl/01_cho.pdf)

Official prefecture birth tabulations classify births by the child's address. Final e-Stat Table 4-3 specifies this convention from 1955 onward, and MHLW's 2017 report states it in the first note to Table 3-3-1. Confirm the convention and residence buckets in each individual monthly Table 2 file's footnotes at acquisition. The combined Table 2 catalogue says the national count includes addresses abroad. MHLW defines that category as events occurring in Japan with an address in another country. [Final e-Stat Table 4-3](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0003411597), [MHLW 2017 report, Table 3-3-1](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/toukei/saikin/hw/jinkou/houkoku17/dl/03.pdf), [combined Table 2](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/dbview?sid=0003411628), [Vital Statistics of Japan 2019](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/english/database/db-hw/dl/81-1a2en.pdf)

## United States and territories

Use the public final CDC WONDER Natality collection covering 2007 onward. Select years 2013 through 2019, all states/DC and all birth characteristics. Group by Year, Month and State, request birth counts, and export the tab-delimited result with the source and query notes. Export a second result by Year and Month for the national monthly controls. State denotes the mother's legal residence at birth. Preserve state and month codes and leave suppressed or unavailable counts unresolved. WONDER's national scope covers births occurring in the 50 states and DC to residents of that same geography. [Final collection](https://wonder.cdc.gov/natality.html), [request](https://wonder.cdc.gov/natality-current.html), [documentation](https://wonder.cdc.gov/wonder/help/natality.html), [state FIPS codes](https://www.census.gov/library/reference/code-lists/ansi/ansi-codes-for-states.html)

For annual controls, transcribe the all-races total by residence from Table 10 of the 2013–2015 *Births: Final Data* PDFs and the all-races-and-origins total from Table 6 of the 2016–2019 PDFs. Read continuation pages, preserve the separate territory rows and check for unavailable cells. The 2019 report's Table 1 lists all seven national controls, in year order: 3,932,181, 3,988,076, 3,978,497, 3,945,875, 3,855,500, 3,791,712 and 3,747,540. The manifest points to each annual report. [2013 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/221896/cdc_221896_DS1.pdf), [2014 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/221898/cdc_221898_DS1.pdf), [2015 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/222008/cdc_222008_DS1.pdf), [2016 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231864/cdc_231864_DS1.pdf), [2017 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231869/cdc_231869_DS1.pdf), [2018 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231883/cdc_231883_DS1.pdf), [2019 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231899/cdc_231899_DS1.pdf)

Keep Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands and other territories in separate source records. WONDER's Region 2 covers New York and New Jersey only. [WONDER geography](https://wonder.cdc.gov/wonder/help/natality.html), [HHS Region 2](https://www.hhs.gov/about/agencies/iea/regional-offices/region-2/index.html), [2018 report](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231883/cdc_231883_DS1.pdf), [2019 report](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231899/cdc_231899_DS1.pdf)

## Stage and validate locally

1. Save original exports and annual reports under `data/raw/natality/`. For each file, record the official landing and download URLs, file ID or query settings, publisher, release/update date, retrieval time in UTC, filename, byte size, SHA-256 and applicable reuse terms. Run `sha256sum -- <original-file>` before opening or transforming the file. Keep the original bytes and query notes with the acquisition receipt. [e-Stat reuse terms](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/en/terms-of-use), [NCHS report metadata and rights](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231899)
2. Verify the native encoding, headings, geography and period labels, and missing-value symbols. Document the mapping when transcribing the first normalized extract or implementing a native parser. Use numeric zero only for a published zero or a symbol whose legend explicitly means zero. Resolve blanks, suppression and unavailable cells before validation.
3. Write normalized UTF-8 files under `data/processed/natality/`. The Japan header is `year,month,location_id,births,male,female`. The US header is `year,month,location_id,births`. Use four-digit years, `01`–`12` for birth months, `annual` for independent published annual rows, two-digit geography codes, and `national` for the country controls. Japan also uses `abroad` and, where separately published, `unknown`. Preserve all 12 months and an annual control for each included geography and residence bucket. Use nonnegative integer counts without separators.
4. Prepare a separate UTF-8 reference CSV for each country under `data/processed/natality/`. Keep the source evidence and reference CSV checksums in the acquisition record.
   - **Japan residence contract** uses `year,stat_infid,catalogue_updated,abroad,unknown` with one row per year. Use the manifest file ID and its ISO update date. For each bucket, enter `present` if it is a required reconciliation component or `absent` if the official vintage establishes that the national total has no such component. Establish the value from the original source documentation and record the supporting label, footnote or table in the acquisition record. A missing or unreadable cell leaves the bucket unresolved.
   - **US annual state controls** use `year,location_id,births,report_url,residence_table` with one row per state/DC and year (357 rows). Transcribe the counts directly from the corresponding final NCHS report table listed in the manifest. Use the exact manifest report URL and table number. Keep these controls independent of the WONDER exports and the normalized extract, and confirm their release and residence scope match the exports.
5. Run the validator with the country-specific reference CSV. It checks each expected location-month, monthly sums against annual rows, national counts against the residence components, and Japan's sex totals. The US check compares each state's annual row with its independent control and checks the national annual value. For Japan, add `--confirm-japan-control-scope` after confirming the revised national series matches all seven files. Record the observed subnational row counts and remaining exceptions before pinning a release.
