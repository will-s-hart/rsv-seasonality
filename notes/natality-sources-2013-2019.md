Expected coverage is 3,948 Japan prefecture-months and 4,284 US state/DC-months. The catalogue entries and published controls were reviewed on 23 November 2026. ## Japan

Use the seven individual final Natality Volume 2 Table 2 CSVs. The catalogue dates are update dates for the tables. Japan's main final tables cover Japanese vital events in Japan and publish events for foreign nationals in Japan, events for Japanese nationals abroad and prior-year events separately. Prefectures classify births by the mother's residence at birth. The national row also includes the foreign-residence bucket, which covers births occurring in Japan when the address is abroad. The resident-population denominator from a separate source may cover additional nationalities. ## United States and territories

Use the public final CDC WONDER Natality collection covering 2007 onward. Select years 2013 through 2019, all states/DC and all birth characteristics. Group by Year, Month and State, request birth counts, and export the tab-delimited result with the source and query notes. Export a second result by Year and Month for the national monthly controls. State denotes the mother's legal residence at birth. Preserve state and month codes and leave suppressed or unavailable counts unresolved. WONDER's national scope covers births occurring in the 50 states and DC to residents of that same geography. [Final collection](https://wonder.cdc.gov/natality.html), [request](https://wonder.cdc.gov/natality-current.html), [documentation](https://wonder.cdc.gov/wonder/help/natality.html), [state FIPS codes](https://www.census.gov/library/reference/code-lists/ansi/ansi-codes-for-states.html)

For annual controls, transcribe the all-races total by residence from Table 10 of the 2013–2015 *Births: Final Data* PDFs and the all-races-and-origins total from Table 6 of the 2016–2019 PDFs. Read continuation pages, preserve the separate territory rows and check for unavailable cells. The 2019 report's Table 1 lists all seven national controls, in year order: 3,932,181, 3,988,076, 3,978,497, 3,945,875, 3,855,500, 3,791,712 and 3,747,540. The manifest points to each annual report. [2013 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/221896/cdc_221896_DS1.pdf), [2014 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/221898/cdc_221898_DS1.pdf), [2015 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/222008/cdc_222008_DS1.pdf), [2016 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231864/cdc_231864_DS1.pdf), [2017 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231869/cdc_231869_DS1.pdf), [2018 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231883/cdc_231883_DS1.pdf), [2019 PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231899/cdc_231899_DS1.pdf)

Keep Puerto Rico, the US Virgin Islands and other territories in separate source records. WONDER's Region 2 covers New York and New Jersey only. HHS Region 2 also includes Puerto Rico and the US Virgin Islands. Monthly territory residence coverage still needs an official aggregate source and a separate validation. The 2018 and 2019 final reports lack US Virgin Islands data, so their annual tables cannot supply a complete baseline control for that territory. [WONDER geography](https://wonder.cdc.gov/wonder/help/natality.html), [HHS Region 2](https://www.hhs.gov/about/agencies/iea/regional-offices/region-2/index.html), [2018 report](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231883/cdc_231883_DS1.pdf), [2019 report](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231899/cdc_231899_DS1.pdf)

## Stage and validate locally

1. Save original exports and annual reports under `data/raw/natality/`. For each file, record the official landing and download URLs, file ID or query settings, publisher, release/update date, retrieval time in UTC, filename, byte size, SHA-256 and applicable reuse terms. Run `sha256sum -- <original-file>` before opening or transforming the file. Keep the original bytes and query notes with the acquisition receipt. [e-Stat reuse terms](https://www.e-stat.go.jp/en/terms-of-use), [NCHS report metadata and rights](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/231899)
2. Verify the native encoding, headings, geography and period labels, and missing-value symbols. Document the mapping when transcribing the first normalized extract or implementing a native parser. Use numeric zero only for a published zero or a symbol whose legend explicitly means zero. Resolve blanks, suppression and unavailable cells before validation.
3. Write normalized UTF-8 files under `data/processed/natality/`. The Japan header is `year,month,location_id,births,male,female`. The US header is `year,month,location_id,births`. Use four-digit years, `01`–`12` for birth months, `annual` for independent published annual rows, two-digit geography codes, and `national` for the country controls. Japan also uses `abroad` and, where separately published, `unknown`. Preserve all 12 months and an annual control for each included geography and residence bucket. Use nonnegative integer counts without separators.
4. Run the validator. It requires one cell per expected key and checks monthly sums against the annual rows, national counts against qualifying residence components, and Japan's sex totals. For Japan, add `--confirm-japan-control-scope` after confirming the revised national series matches all seven files. The US national annual values are checked by default. Record the observed subnational row counts and remaining exceptions before pinning a release.
